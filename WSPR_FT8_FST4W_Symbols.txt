STATION AB0E / GRID EM28 - ALL OPERATING MODES & TIMING SPECIFICATIONS
Rig: MEPT Beacon (Arduino UNO R4 WiFi + Si5351A + Nextion + 8-Relay + GPS)

1. STATION PARAMETERS & TIMING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Callsign:           AB0E
Grid Locator:       EM28
Transmit Power:     7 dBm (approx 5.0 mW EIRP)
CW/FSK/DFCW ID:     "AB0E EM28"
Supported Timing:   WiFi (Internet NTP) and GPS (NMEA @ 9600 baud) ONLY.
Frequency Calib:    GPS Module Reference / GPS-Disciplined (Replaced WWV/WWVH)

2. MODE SPECIFICATIONS & TIMING WINDOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Mode 1] WSPR-2:
  - 4-FSK modulation, tone spacing: 1.4648 Hz, baud rate: 1.4648 baud.
  - Total duration: 110.6 seconds (transmitted during even UTC minutes).
  - 162-tone sequence dynamically generated or pre-computed for "AB0E EM28 7".

[Mode 2] FT8:
  - 8-FSK modulation, tone spacing: 6.25 Hz, baud rate: 6.25 baud.
  - Transmission duration: 12.64 seconds per sequence.
  - Transmit intervals: :00, :15, :30, and :45 seconds past the minute.
  - 79 channel symbols (Costas sync [3,1,4,0,6,5,2] + CRC-14 0x2757).

[Mode 3] FST4W-120:
  - 4-FSK modulation, 120-second sequence (160 tones, duration: 116.8s).
  - Tone spacing: 1.4648 Hz. Transmitted during even UTC minutes.
  - Generated via: fst4sim "AB0E EM28 7" 120 1500 0.0 0.1 1.0 0 99 F

[Mode 4] CW (Continuous Wave):
  - Standard Morse code at 12 WPM: "AB0E EM28".
  - Keying: Si5351 CLK0 on/off keying via register CLK_ENABLE_CONTROL.

[Mode 5] FSKCW:
  - Frequency Shift Keying CW with 4–6 Hz frequency shift between Mark and Space.
  - Non-stop carrier transmission (ideal for grabbing on grabbers/spectrograms).

[Mode 6] QRSS (Slow-speed CW):
  - QRSS3 protocol: 3-second dits, 9-second dahs.
  - Callsign and Grid "AB0E EM28" visually readable on QRSS grabbers worldwide.

[Mode 7] DFCW (Dual Frequency CW):
  - Dit and Dah have equal element lengths (3 seconds each).
  - Dits transmitted on base frequency f0; Dahs transmitted on f0 + 6 Hz.
  - Doubled transmission speed compared to QRSS with visual clarity.

[Mode 8] Hellschreiber (Feld-Hell):
  - Direct facsimile transmission @ 122.5 baud (4.08 ms per pixel).
  - 7x7 pixel font matrix transmitting column by column for "AB0E EM28".
  - Human readable on amateur Hellschreiber receivers (FLDIGI).

3. FREQUENCY CALIBRATION VIA GPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Replaced legacy WWV / WWVH acoustic/manual beat method with GPS reference.
  - In FREQ CALIB mode, pressing PB2 initiates a GPS lock verification.
  - Emits 25.000000 MHz reference signal on CLK1 with fine digital PPB adjustments.
